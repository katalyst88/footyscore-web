Wear OS testing

Help test Footy Scores.

The Wear OS app is in closed testing. Join the tester group, accept the invitation, then install it on your watch.

Join the tester group

Free while testing.

Three steps

  1. Join the group

    Open the tester group and click Join group. Use the same Google account you use on Google Play.

  2. Accept the invitation

    Open the opt in page and click Become a tester.

  3. Install it on your watch

    Open the Play Store on the watch, search Footy Scores, and tap Install.

    It installs straight to the watch. There is no phone app.

What to look at

Anything you notice is worth sending. These are the parts most likely to be wrong.

  • Your club: Set it in Settings. Does its match sit at the top, in its colours?
  • Live scores: During a game, do the score and clock keep up?
  • The tile: Swipe from the watch face. Is it showing the right match?
  • Alerts: Turn on goals for your club. Do you feel the buzz?
  • Spoiler free: Turn on hidden scores. Does anything leak a result?
  • Battery: Anything unusual on a day your club plays.

If it will not install

  • Check the watch is on wi fi, or has its phone nearby.
  • Make sure the watch uses the same Google account that joined the group.
  • Give it up to an hour after joining. Google Play can be slow to notice.
  • Search the watch's own Play Store, not your phone's.

Telling me what you found

Email watchwalkssupport@gmail.com.

What helps most: your watch model, what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A photo of the screen is ideal.

To leave the test, open the opt in page and click Leave the programme.
